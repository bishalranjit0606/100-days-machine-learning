Day 8

Applications of Machine Learning

48

ML in Everyday Life

Look around and you see AI everywhere: chatbots, movie recommendations, and more. Machine learning is part of daily life.

Many people think ML is only what we use directly as customers. But a lot of it is B2B (business to business). B2B often brings more profit than B2C (business to customer).

A famous line: if you are not paying for the product, you are the product. Our data is what gets sold.

49

Retail

If a retail company does not use ML, that can be a major drawback for sales. Competitors with better insight will move ahead.

We collect sales data and find insight: should we stock this product or not? ML helps decision making in retail.

Stores track buying behavior. They may ask for your phone number first. They learn what you buy, like health products after you join a gym. If you shop at certain supermarkets, your data can be sold for target marketing.

Association rule mining finds relations between products. Example: if people buy milk, they often buy eggs too.

50

Banking

When you apply for a loan, the bank analyzes your profile. It compares you to people who did not pay back. If there is an 80% match, they may not give you the loan.

Other banking uses of ML:

  • Deciding where to open new branches.
  • Stock market analysis.
51

Ride Sharing

In ride sharing apps, the price goes up in some areas. This is surge pricing. It happens when demand is high and drivers are few.

ML helps balance demand and supply so prices and availability make sense.

Example: mango price in Nepal goes high when demand increases. Same idea: more demand, higher price.

52

Manufacturing

Tesla uses automation heavily. Robotic arms build the cars.

Machines do not break all at once. Damage happens gradually. ML helps find problems early using sensor data like heat. This is called predictive maintenance.

How predictive maintenance works.

  1. Sensors Heat, vibration, and more.
  2. ML model Spots early warning signs.
  3. Fix early Before a full breakdown.
53

Social Media

Social media runs on algorithms. ML decides what you see in your feed.

Sentiment analysis is widely used today. It finds what people feel or think from text and posts.

It matters in politics, sports, and anywhere we want to know what a crowd will do or what people are thinking. Chatbots also use these ideas.

  • Retail.

    Stock, pricing, and buying patterns.

  • Banking.

    Loans, branches, and markets.

  • Ride sharing.

    Demand, supply, and surge pricing.

  • Manufacturing.

    Automation and predictive maintenance.

  • Social media.

    Feeds, sentiment, and chatbots.

Day 8 takeaways

  • ML is everywhere, often behind the scenes in B2B products.
  • Five major sectors: retail, banking, ride sharing, manufacturing, and social media.
  • Your data powers many free apps. ML turns that data into decisions.

Knowing where ML is used helps you see real world problems worth solving.
