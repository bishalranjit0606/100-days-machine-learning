Day 46

Gen AI vs Agentic AI

Gen AI makes new content. Agentic AI works toward a goal. We walk from a plain chatbot to RAG, tools, then an agent, using a hiring story.

616

What Is Gen AI

Gen AI creates new content. That can be an image, audio, code, or video. The result often feels like a human made it. The best part of Gen AI is that it mimics humans.

Gen AI is constantly improving.

617

Products in this family include ChatGPT, models that make images, LLMs that write code, text to speech models, and video generation models. The cards below are that set.

ChatGPT

A chat product built on a large language model.

Image models

Make new pictures from a prompt.

Code LLMs

Write and edit program code.

Text to speech

Turn written text into spoken audio.

Video models

Generate new video.

618

Gen AI vs Traditional AI

Traditional AI finds patterns in data and gives a prediction. Classification is one case. Look at an email and decide if it is spam or not. The model takes input and output pairs, learns the pattern, then predicts. Regression is the same idea: input and output, find the pattern, then predict a number.

Gen AI does not only find a pattern between one input and one output. If you send it 1000 images of a cat, it tries to learn the distribution of that data so it can generate a new sample. The steps below are that idea.

Learn the spread, then make a new sample

  1. Many cats You give about 1000 cat images.
  2. Distribution The model learns how those images are spread.
  3. New sample It can generate a new cat image that was not in the set.

The table below puts traditional AI next to Gen AI.

Traditional AI Gen AI
Find patterns. Predict a label or a number. Learn the distribution. Generate a new sample.
Spam or not. Regression from input to output. A new cat image, a new sentence, a new clip of audio.

620

Hiring Without Gen AI

Suppose we are hiring a backend engineer. You are working as an HR engineer. Without Gen AI you do every step yourself. The chain below is that path.

Hire a backend engineer by hand

  1. Write the JD Create the job description.
  2. Post it Put it on LinkedIn or Recruit Nepal.
  3. Shortlist Cut a large pile, even 1000 people, down to a few.
  4. Interview Talk with the shortlisted people.
  5. Offer Roll out the offer letter.
  6. Onboard Bring the hire into the company.
621

Hiring With a Chatbot

Now the company gives you a chatbot. You still run hiring, but Gen AI helps write. You prompt it for a JD from your details. It can suggest posting on Recruit Nepal. It can give advice for shortlisting, such as people who know the path. It can draft emails, interview questions, and the offer letter. Without Gen AI you write all of that yourself.

The table below is each step with the chatbot beside it.

Step Chatbot help
Job description Write a JD from the details you give.
Posting Suggest where to post, such as Recruit Nepal.
Shortlisting Give advice, such as look for the right path knowledge.
Email Draft the email to a candidate.
Interview List questions to ask.
Offer letter Help write the offer letter.
622

Why That Chatbot Is Not Enough

Is the chatbot the best solution? No. We can still do better. You must prompt it every time. It only replies. It does not have lasting memory. It forgets the data in 3 days. It cannot act on its own. It can write an email, but it does not get access to post that email. It also gives generic answers, not answers that match this company.

Prompt only

It answers the prompt. It does not run the next step unless you ask.

No memory

It forgets in 3 days.

No action

It can draft an email. It cannot send or post it.

Generic

The advice is general, not your company rules.

A RAG Based Chatbot

We need to give the chatbot our own files. The cards below are that set. Past JDs. Examples of high performing JDs. Hiring strategies and best practices. An internal salary band. An internal checklist to verify the hire. An onboarding checklist and offer letter samples.

Past JDs

Old job descriptions from this company.

Strong JDs

Examples of high performing job posts.

Hiring practice

Strategies and best practices for hiring.

Salary band

Internal pay range for the role.

Hire checklist

Internal steps to verify the hire.

Onboarding

Onboarding checklist and offer letter samples.

That is called RAG. A RAG based chatbot can give company based answers. It still does not fix every issue. It is still reactive. It still has no memory. It still cannot take action.

624

A Tool Augmented Chatbot

The next improvement is action. The chatbot can send the email or post the JD. We integrate it with the tools below. LinkedIn. A resume parser. A calendar tool for interviews. A mail API. This chatbot is called a tool augmented chatbot.

LinkedIn

Post the JD or work with that portal.

Resume parser

Read resumes so shortlisting can use the file.

Calendar

Book interview times.

Mail API

Send the email, not only draft it.

Tools solve the action gap. A few problems remain. It is still reactive. It still has no memory. It still cannot adapt.

625

Agentic AI

Now we need it to be proactive, to have memory, and to adapt. That chatbot is an AI agent. We call it an agentic AI chatbot. You only state the end goal. You say you want to hire a backend developer with 4 years of experience. The agent plans, processes, and adapts on its own. It can monitor jobs, screen and find a strong candidate, and schedule the calendar.

That is why we use agentic AI instead of stopping at Gen AI. The other gaps are closed.

626

The Path From Gen AI to Agents

The line is Gen AI, then RAG plus Gen AI, then tools, then agentic AI. The chain below is that path.

Four stages

  1. Gen AI Create text, images, and other content.
  2. RAG Gen AI plus your company files.
  3. Tools Send mail, post a JD, open a calendar.
  4. Agentic AI Reach the goal. Plan, act, and adapt.

Gen AI is only for content, such as text or an image. Agentic AI tries to achieve the goal any how. Gen AI is reactive. We guide every step. Agentic AI is proactive. A human is there for approval. Gen AI is the building block of agentic AI. Gen AI is the capability. Agentic AI is the behavior.

Gen AI Agentic AI
Create content: text, image, and more. Achieve the goal any how.
Reactive. You guide every step. Proactive. A human gives approval.
A capability. The building block. A behavior built on that capability.
627

Day 46 takeaways

Day 46 takeaways

  • Gen AI creates new content that can feel human made. ChatGPT, image models, code LLMs, text to speech, and video models sit in this family.
  • Traditional AI predicts from input and output pairs. Gen AI learns a distribution so it can make a new sample, such as a new cat image.
  • Gen AI helps writing, software, support, education, and design. It keeps improving.
  • A hiring chatbot can draft a JD, emails, questions, and an offer. It still needs a prompt, forgets in 3 days, cannot act, and stays generic.
  • RAG adds company files. Tools add actions like mail and calendars. Both can still be reactive and without memory.
  • An agent is proactive, has memory, and adapts. You give the goal, such as hire a backend developer with 4 years of experience.
  • The path is Gen AI, then RAG, then tools, then agentic AI. Gen AI is the capability. Agentic AI is the behavior.
