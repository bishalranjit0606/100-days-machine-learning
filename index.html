Why this website exists

ML foundations, then Gen AI apps

This is 100 Days of Machine Learning for the community. Each day adds one topic for 100 days.

Days 1 to 19 teach ML judgment: the life cycle, data roles, framing problems, and working with real data. From Day 20 the series continues into generative AI and LangChain, the same way many teams ship products today.

It is for beginners and intermediate learners who want proficiency with data, decisions, and LLM apps, not a long list of algorithm names. New market skills keep arriving one day at a time.

ML life cycle

Frame the problem, gather data, and think end to end.

Data skills

CSV, JSON, APIs, web data, NumPy, and Pandas.

Problem framing

Turn a business goal into a clear learning task.

Gen AI map

Foundation models, and user vs builder paths.

LangChain apps

Models, prompts, and structured output live now.

Next days

More shipping skills arrive day by day as we publish.

What this series is not

This is not a deep catalog of every classic ML algorithm. Excellent resources already cover that. Here you learn how the pieces connect: ML foundations first, then Gen AI apps you can ship.
